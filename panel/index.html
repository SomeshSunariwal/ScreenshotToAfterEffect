Panel controls

Zoom in
Ctrl + scroll up
Zoom out
Ctrl + scroll down
Pan
Drag with the middle mouse button.
Select boxes
Drag across boxes with Image Area off.
Resize on one axis
Hold Shift and drag a resize handle horizontally or vertically.
Lock
Right-click a box or selection and choose Lock.
Delete
Press Delete on selected boxes or use the right-click menu.
Undo box action
Ctrl + Z
Redo box action
Ctrl + Shift + Z
Crop
Turn on Image Area and drag over the image.
From comp
Select one still-image layer, then click Use Selected Image.
